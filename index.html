The 3D pathfinding & volumetric navigation plugin for Unreal Engine 5

★ 5.0 on FabSparse Voxel OctreeGPU-built navigation85 AI nodes10,000+ agentsMoving-obstacle predictionMultiplayer replicationBlueprint & C++

LCM 3D Navigation AI brings true 3D pathfinding to Unreal Engine 5 — flying AI pathfinding for drones, dragons, spaceships, helicopters and underwater agents that Recast navmesh simply cannot express. Unreal's Recast navmesh answers one question: where can I stand? It flattens your level into walkable polygons — exactly right for a soldier, completely wrong for a dragon. LCM 3D Navigation AI answers a different one: where is there empty air? It carves the whole playable volume into a Sparse Voxel Octree and paths through the empty nodes, so open sky costs almost nothing and detail appears only where geometry actually is.

  1. 01Sparse Voxel OctreeThe level's empty air is subdivided only where geometry exists — one node covers open sky, so cost tracks clutter, not level size. Voxels go down to 0.5 cm, so the same plugin navigates a 120 cm aquarium at 1 cm and a streamed open world. Drop a single LcmNavigationManagerSVO per level and it owns the octree, the pathfinder and every query.
  2. 02Three solvers, three smoothing modesA* for raw throughput, Theta* for true any-angle routes, Lazy Theta* for near-Theta* quality at near-A* cost — multi-threaded and deterministic. Then Raw, Linear Shortcut or Curved Spline smoothing — validated against geometry, so a smoothed path never cuts a wall.
  3. 03GPU-built navigation that checks itselfVoxelization, the octree and its links are built on the GPU on D3D12, D3D11 and Vulkan. New in 3.6: each machine's GPU proves its voxels before it builds navigation — a capability check, a self-test remembered per GPU and driver, and sampled audits while it runs. A GPU that fails voxelizes on the CPU for that session, and the log says why.
  4. 04Macro / micro routing & infinite worldsLong routes solve coarsely across chunks, then finely within them. Finite worlds build once or bake to an asset; infinite worlds stream chunks around agents and throw them away behind, with pre-baked chunks and a long-range graph that plans beyond loaded geometry.
  5. 05Agents that see moving obstacles comingNew in 3.6: Fly To predicts every moving obstacle and times its pass. It learns a gate's or a piston's cycle, turns a spinning blade with it, eases off, waits for a gap or takes a detour — and dodges what it cannot outrun. Moving geometry still restamps the octree, so routes re-plan live.
  6. 06A behaviour for every job — 85 AI nodesFly To, Patrol, Orbit, Follow, Hover, Take Off, Land (even on a rolling ship's deck), Perch, Scan Area, Engage Target, Find Cover, Flee and Investigate — 31 Behaviour Tree nodes, 41 StateTree nodes and 13 Mass StateTree nodes at full parity. One Fly To Location Blueprint node flies any pawn with no AI Controller, and Unreal's own Move To and EQS plan through the octree.
  7. 0710,000+ agents through Mass ECSEight Mass traits: per-agent asynchronous pathfinding, or one shared CPU flow field sampled O(1) by an entire swarm. Mass StateTree lets crowds perch, scan, engage, investigate and flee; schooling, Hold Avoidance and Contact Push keep dense crowds apart. A per-agent GPU solver is in experimental validation — coming as a free update.
  8. 08Tactical EQS in true 3D5 generators, 8 tests and 4 contexts that score real volume — volumetric line-of-sight, cover, threat exposure, height advantage, landing sites and true path distance — so a sniper perch is chosen in 3D, not on a floor plane.
  9. 096-DOF flight & swim locomotionNavigation gives guidance; LcmFlightMovementComponent turns it into a body. Multirotor drone, Jet / Fixed-Wing, Bird (flap-glide) and Fish (undulatory) archetypes with thrust, lift, drag, buoyancy and attitude PID. Fish hold depth, school in layers and beat their tails with their speed; flight follows the level's gravity.
  10. 10Cost areas, priced per agentMark volumes as storms, preferred lanes, toll gates or no-fly zones with stock NavArea classes. A stock Navigation Query Filter decides what each area costs each agent — or forbids it — and every planner routes by them, across streamed chunk seams too.
  11. 11Server-authoritative multiplayer replicationClients request a route, the server solves it and delivers it back through Client_DeliverPath; moving obstacles replay their nav footprint to every peer over a multicast. A headless validation suite (-LcmMpTest) exercises the sanity, replicated-request, multicast-transport and digest-chain paths, and CPU compute stays bit-deterministic for replays and lockstep.
  12. 12Built for real projectsDynamic obstacle restamping, AI Perception threat tracking, hybrid Recast + SVO levels, nav links, area modifiers, a setup wizard, a one-click health check and a live Gameplay Debugger — plus 57 free demo levels you download from inside the editor.

Works with every Unreal AI system

Whichever way your team authors behaviour, LCM 3D Navigation AI plugs into it — the same navigation data, three authoring surfaces.

Behaviour Tree

21 tasks, 5 decorators and 5 services — FlyTo, Patrol, Land, Perch, Engage Target, Find Cover, Flee, Investigate, line-of-sight and chunk-loaded checks — alongside seven documented stock-node patterns showing where an LCM node earns its place.

StateTree

25 tasks, 8 conditions and 8 evaluators at full parity with the Behaviour Tree nodes, so state-driven agents get identical routing and behaviours without a Behaviour Tree in the stack.

Mass Entity

Eight ECS traits — per-agent pathfinding or a shared flow field — plus 10 Mass StateTree tasks and 3 evaluators, so a crowd of 10,000+ entities can perch, scan, engage, investigate and flee.

Plus a Fly To Location Blueprint node that flies any pawn with no AI Controller, and Unreal's own Move To, AI Move To and EQS planning through the octree.

Platform support

Supported on desktop, mobile and current-gen consoles — Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — with the C++ core built to the same standard on every target. Consoles compile from source with your own platform SDK.
